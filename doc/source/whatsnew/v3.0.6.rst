.. _whatsnew_306.regressions:

Fixed regressions
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Bug in :meth:`Series.str.split`, :meth:`Series.str.rsplit`, :meth:`Series.str.partition`, and :meth:`Series.str.rpartition` on :class:`CategoricalDtype` Series with ``expand=False`` returning stringified lists/tuples instead of actual lists/tuples (:issue:`66341`)
- Bug in :meth:`Timestamp.tz_convert` and :meth:`Timestamp.tz_localize` and their :class:`Index` and :class:`Series` counterparts returning a UTC offset a few minutes off for dates before the specified timezone adopted a standard time (typically early 20th century); this only affected older systems whose ``tzdata`` was compiled with a toolchain from between 2013 and 2018, such as Amazon Linux 2 and RHEL 8 and its derivatives (:issue:`67066`)
